How to Use

Get started in 3 simple steps — works for both listed NSE stocks and SME / Mainboard IPOs.

1

Choose Your Mode

Open the Analyzer and pick Results & AGM for listed NSE stocks, or IPO Report (SME & Regular) for upcoming and recently listed IPOs.

2

Search & Set Window

Search any NSE symbol (like RELIANCE, TCS, HDFCBANK), select RESULTS, AGM, or a CUSTOM date, and set your before/after trading-day window (e.g. T-20 to T+20).

3

Review Charts & Export

Inspect the average pre/post-event move, win rate, alpha vs. NIFTY 50, interactive price & volume charts, or download the full breakdown as a CSV.

What You Get

NSE Stocks
  • Pre-Event vs. Post-Event Returns: See how a stock typically moves leading up to and after Results or AGMs.
