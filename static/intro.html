How to Use

Switch between the two tabs at the top of the Analyzer depending on what you want to research.

Mode 1: Results & AGM Analyzer

Listed NSE Stocks
  1. 1
    Search an NSE Stock: Type any symbol or company name (e.g., RELIANCE, TCS, HDFCBANK) and select it from the dropdown.
  2. 2
    Pick Event Type & Window: Choose Results, AGM, or Custom (enter comma-separated YYYY-MM-DD dates). Set trading days Before (T-) and After (T+) (1–120 days) and how many past Events to study (4, 8, 12, or 20).
  3. 3
    Analyze & Drill Down: Click Analyze. Click any row in the Range Guide to test a better window, tap any row in the Event-by-Event Breakdown to plot its exact ₹ price & volume chart, or click CSV in the header to export.

Mode 2: IPO Report (SME & Regular)

Mainboard & SME
  1. 1
    Filter the Indian IPO List: Filter live IPOs by Type (All / Regular / SME), Exchange (NSE / BSE), Status (Open / Allotment / Upcoming / Listed), or search by IPO name.
  2. 2
    Click Analyze or Predict: Click Analyze on any listed/active IPO for an instant automated report, or click Predict to open the editable RHP/financial input panel (even test with Load Example).
  3. 3
    Customize & Evaluate: In Predict mode, enter Upper Price, Lot Size, Issue Size, Fresh Issue %, Post-IPO EPS, 3-Year Revenue & PAT, Net Worth, Debt, QIB/NII/Retail subscription, and NSE Peers — then click Generate Prediction & Report.

Good to Know

Is VBP Research free to use?

Yes — 100% free and public with no login, paywall, or subscription required.

Why is the IPO analyzer GMP-free?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) is unregulated and noisy. VBP Research focuses on verifiable financials, valuation multiples, peer P/E, and official QIB/NII/Retail subscription data.

Is this SEBI-registered advice?

No. All charts, scores, setups, and estimates are strictly for educational and independent research purposes. Always do your own due diligence.
